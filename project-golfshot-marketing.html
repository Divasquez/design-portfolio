Golfshot GPS
Marketing

Work Summary

Golfshot is a mobile and smartwatch application created by Shortzoom Software that helps users play golf by providing GPS course maps, tracking scores, and providing personalized statistics.

From 2017 to 2022, I worked at Shotzoom as a UI/UX designer and in the Marketing department, creating mostly pieces of digital advertising such as social media posts, email campaigns, and our brand website.

I worked with the social media manager and marketing manager to curate meaningful content for our audience and shape the brand’s online presence. We also created targeted marketing strategies to drive growth, engagement, and visibility.

Nurturing an attentive and dedicated audience of users was also important, as they were crucial to getting real-world feedback on the app from beta tests and new feature launches.

Project Highlights

Golfshot was shown at the 2021 Apple Event for the new Auto Shot Tracking feature.

Planned and scripted one of our first professional ads for Golfshot.

Launched in 2018, designed alongside Dan Fournier, the lead designer at the time
