Business Cards
Noland Araugh - P1

Project Summary

Noland Arbaugh is "the world’s first human with a Neuralink brain chip. Paralyzed from the shoulders down after a tragic accident, he now controls a computer with his mind and inspires audiences around the world to rethink what it means to be human” (iamp1.com). He is currently working as a public speaker, touching on topics from his faith journey to the impact of the latest technological frontiers on humanity.

Noland attends many conferences where he speaks and connects with scientists, entrepreneurs, and world leaders, and he needed a business card to reflect him and his story.

Drafts

These are the original drawings I first presented for the project. We agreed that the cards should be simple but striking. Noland wanted to include an original illustration of a brain with a microchip or a brain with wires.

Final Design

Noland loved the draft, so I proceeded to just clean up the design, create the final illustration, and select printing options.

These cards were printed on suede paper with spot UV on the brain illustration, which reflects light nicely without compromising legibility.
