Work Summary

Since April 2023, I’ve been the Marketing Manager for a busy Phoenix real estate team. My role blends design, content creation, project management, and day-to-day marketing operations to support every stage of the sales process.

I create a wide range of marketing assets for every property, including flyers, booklets, direct mail pieces, social media graphics, and promotional videos. I also manage our social media presence by planning content, scheduling posts, engaging with followers, and maintaining a cohesive brand identity across platforms.

Beyond creative work, I update website content, send newsletters about local events and news, coordinate with agents, and track every listing throughout its lifecycle to ensure marketing stays accurate, organized, and on schedule.

Social Media Presence

I develop and manage a social media strategy focused on creating engaging, educational content that highlights our agents’ expertise while remaining approachable and authentic. My goal is to present our realtors as knowledgeable professionals without the content ever feeling overly sales-driven.

I share interesting and relatable content about life in Phoenix to help build a stronger connection between our audience, our community, and our brand. We also create short, easy-to-understand videos that explain the home buying and selling process, with the intention of making real estate feel less intimidating and more accessible.

Social media is also an important channel for marketing our listings. I promote properties using professional photography, walk-through videos, and targeted campaigns designed to increase visibility and attract genuine interest.

Overall, my approach is to build a thoughtful, consistent social presence that builds trust, showcases expertise, and supports real business goals.
