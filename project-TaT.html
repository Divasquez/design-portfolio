Thick as Thieves
Book Cover

Project Summary

Thick as Thieves is the first novel written by Myranda Berardi. It’s a modern action-and-adventure tale about a young woman who is kidnapped and eventually rescued by a man she cannot fully trust. The two search for the woman’s family while falling for each other and untangling their past.

My task was to create the book cover based on the symbolism and themes that run through the story.

Drafts

Myranda and her editor sent me a rough mockup of what they wanted the cover to look like, along with the specific flowers and snake species to include. I created a rough sketch of the cover, selecting a color palette that would harmonize the colors of all the elements.

Final Design

I rendeded the snake and flowers illustration, and after some more feedback we added the elment of the arrows since those were important to the story. With a finished illlustration, we then chose fonts for the title and author to finalize the composition.
