Lauren's Loft
Branding

Project Summary

Lauren’s Loft is a small business founded by Lauren Bracken, specializing in handmade bags and leather accessories. Lauren reached out to me when she was getting her business off the ground, wanting a logo and custom iconography to bring her vision to life across her website, social media, and product packaging.

Rooted in Western influences, Lauren’s Loft draws inspiration from cowboys, the Southwest desert, and Native American-inspired patterns. Together, we developed a visual identity that balanced vintage character with a fresh, modern feel—feminine without being overly delicate, and authentic to the independent spirit of the Old West. Below are the logo, iconography, and examples of their use.

Logomark and Bag Tag

As a creative artisan, Lauren already had a strong sense of branding for her business. She sent me a sketch for a logo using horseshoes to create a flower-like design, and that became the base of the design. I also created a name tag to that would be added in leather to all the bags, this was was a simple yet recognizable tag with the brand’s name and mission statement.
